PostItem · mobile layout — three strategies

Stack source: PostListItem.tsx & PostFeaturedCard.tsx. The meta row currently uses flex items-center justify-between on a single line. On a 360–390 px viewport the left meta cluster and the right stats cluster compete for width, each child collapses to its narrowest possible width, and CJK characters wrap one-per-line. Each column below shows a fix.
